> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY for the hackathon prototype. Not an actual telecom regulation or HCLTech policy.

## 1. Data minimisation
Only the facts needed to write a message are shared with the writing system: first name, tenure, contract type, billing amount and due date, reminder stage and approved offer details. Full names, email addresses and other identifiers are not sent.

## 2. Protected characteristics
Gender, age, senior citizen status, marital status, dependents, education, income and credit score are never used to decide segments, offers, reminder severity or any treatment. They are not stored in the customer engagement database.

## 3. Personal data in emails
Emails must not contain card numbers, national ID numbers, other customers' details, or contact details other than the official support channels and the customer's own address.

## 4. Customer isolation
Answers and messages about one customer must only use that customer's verified data. Information about one customer must never appear in another customer's message or answer.

## 5. Audit logs
Audit logs store customer IDs and the minimum evidence needed to explain a decision. Names and email addresses are redacted.
