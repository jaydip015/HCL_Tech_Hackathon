> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY for the hackathon prototype. Not an actual telecom regulation or HCLTech policy.

## 1. Purpose
Payment reminders help customers settle overdue bills while treating them with respect. A reminder is a service message, not a promotion.

## 2. Reminder stages
Days overdue are counted from the bill due date. Stage 1 (1 to 15 days overdue) is a courteous reminder. Stage 2 (16 to 30 days overdue) is a firm but respectful reminder. Stage 3 (more than 30 days overdue) is a formal notice. Boundaries are inclusive: day 15 is stage 1, day 16 is stage 2, day 30 is stage 2 and day 31 is stage 3.

## 3. Required content
Every reminder must state the exact outstanding amount and the due date from the billing system, and must include the support phone number and the billing dispute link so the customer can question the bill.

## 4. Prohibited content
Reminders must never include offers, discounts or promotional language. They must never threaten legal action, use phrases such as "final warning", shame the customer, or make claims that are not supported by verified billing data.

## 5. Data problems
If billing data is missing or contradictory, for example a bill marked PAID with an outstanding amount, no reminder is generated and the case is escalated to a human.

## 6. Customer has paid
If the customer pays before a reminder is sent, the reminder must be cancelled.
