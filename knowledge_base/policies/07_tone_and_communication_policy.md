> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY for the hackathon prototype. Not an actual telecom regulation or HCLTech policy.

## 1. General principles
Every email must be clear, respectful, short and personal. Use the customer's first name. Use plain language and one clear call to action. Never mention internal labels such as segments, reason codes or churn scores.

## 3.1 Appreciation (warm)
For consistent customers, thank them sincerely for paying on time and staying active. A warm tone uses words such as thank you, appreciate and grateful.

## 3.2 Reward (warm)
For loyal customers, thank them for their years with ExampleTel and present the approved reward exactly as approved.

## 3.3 Re-engagement and reactivation (friendly)
For customers with lower activity, use a friendly, low-pressure tone. Say that you would love to help them get more from their plan and that support is here to help. Do not guilt the customer.

## 3.4 Reminders
Stage 1 is courteous: a polite note that a payment appears to be outstanding. Stage 2 is firm but respectful: clearly ask the customer to pay or contact support. Stage 3 is a formal notice: state the facts formally and politely. All stages say please and offer help.

## 4. Things to avoid
Avoid ALL CAPS, more than three exclamation marks, urgency tricks such as "last chance" or "act now or", absolute promises such as "guaranteed", and any threat.
