> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY for the hackathon prototype. Not an actual telecom regulation or HCLTech policy.

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how ExampleTel recognises and rewards long-standing customers. Loyalty communication should make customers feel valued, never pressured.

## 2. Who is a loyal customer
A customer is treated as loyal when their tenure is 36 months or more, as defined in the Customer Segmentation Policy section 2.4. Loyalty is based only on tenure and verified account history. Age, gender, income or any other protected characteristic must never be used to decide loyalty or rewards.

## 3. Loyalty rewards
Loyal customers with valid marketing consent may receive one approved loyalty reward from the offer catalog per message. The current approved reward is LOYALTY_001 (Loyalty Rewards, 10% discount, valid for the next billing cycle). Rewards must be stated exactly as approved, including value, terms and expiry. If no approved loyalty offer is available, send an appreciation message without an offer instead of inventing one.

## 4. Loyal customers with an overdue bill
When a loyal customer also has an overdue bill, the payment reminder takes priority and no reward may be included. The reminder may acknowledge the customer's long relationship respectfully.

## 5. Tone
Loyalty messages use a warm tone: thank the customer for their time with ExampleTel and mention their tenure where it is verified.
