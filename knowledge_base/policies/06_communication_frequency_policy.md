> SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY for the hackathon prototype. Not an actual telecom regulation or HCLTech policy.

## 1. Cooldown periods
No marketing email may be sent within 7 days of any previous contact with the customer. No payment reminder may be sent within 5 days of the previous contact. Blocked messages use the code RECENT_CONTACT.

## 2. Duplicate outreach
The same template, or identical content, must not be sent to the same customer within 30 days.

## 3. No bulk sending
Messages are prepared as drafts. Every message requires human approval before delivery. Automated bulk sending is not permitted.
